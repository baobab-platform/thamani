The source PDF is held by the sponsor and is deliberately not committed. It lists directors with identity numbers, dates of birth and residential and postal addresses, which are personal information. The certificate also carries a tax number; it is deliberately not recorded here either, because the registry needs only the identity facts above.

## How this was checked

An AI-generated transcription of the certificate was compared with the certificate itself on 10 October 2026. Only facts visible on the certificate are recorded above. Two items in that transcription are **not** on the certificate and are therefore not recorded:

- A statement that the enterprise is "associated with Nabhold Group Africa board resolutions". The certificate shows no shareholder, parent or group relationship.
- Incorporation tracking and reference numbers. The certificate shows no tracking number. They presumably come from other incorporation documents, which were not available to check.

## Not established by this evidence

- Ownership. The certificate lists no shareholders. Whether Thamani Global is a subsidiary of Nabhold Group Africa, and in what percentage, is not shown. The group role recorded in the Shared legal-entity registry remains a group label, not verified legal-person status.
- Beneficial ownership filing, annual return, VAT and PAYE registration, POPIA and Information Officer status.
- Director status after 13/04/2026. Later changes are not covered.
- Any previous CIPC enterprise number or other name.
- That the operating business behind this repository (Thamani) is carried on by this legal entity rather than by another entity.

## Independence note

Sign-off should be given by someone other than a director named on the certificate where that is possible. If it is not possible, record who gave it and in what capacity.
